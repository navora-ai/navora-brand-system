# Brand Constitution

## Mission

NAVORA exists to transform clinical decision-making through evidence-based intelligence infrastructure. Every design decision serves **clarity**, **trust**, and **clinical precision**.

## Brand Pillars

### 1. Clinical Authority
NAVORA communicates with the gravitas of a peer-reviewed journal, not a consumer app. Every pixel earns the trust of clinicians who stake patient outcomes on the data it surfaces.

### 2. Evidence-First
Gold (`#B9954E`) is reserved exclusively for evidence markers — literature references, citation badges, and clinical verification indicators. It is never decorative.

### 3. Calm Precision
Healthcare UIs must reduce cognitive load, not add to it. NAVORA uses a restrained palette (Navy 65–70%, Ivory 25–30%, Gold max 5%) with generous white space and deliberate typographic hierarchy.

### 4. Institutional Permanence
The brand conveys stability and longevity. Serif display type (Newsreader) anchors the product in academic credibility. No trend-chasing, no gratuitous animations, no decorative gradients.

## Design Principles

1. **Hierarchy is non-negotiable** — Every screen must have exactly one focal point. Use type scale, color weight, and spatial dominance to direct attention.
2. **Data is sacred** — Clinical values render in `IBM Plex Mono` at sufficient size. No truncation, no ambiguous abbreviation, no color-only encoding.
3. **Modes, not themes** — Light and Dark modes are functional (clinical setting vs. reading), not cosmetic. Both must pass WCAG AA contrast.
4. **Components over patterns** — If it appears twice, it is a component. If a component varies, it uses variants with explicit tokens, not ad-hoc overrides.
5. **Status communicates, never decorates** — Sage = verified, Blue = new/imported, Gold = action needed, Red = safety/conflict. These are fixed assignments.

## Brand Voice

- **Professional but not cold** — Address clinicians as peers, not customers
- **Precise but not jargon-heavy** — Use clinical terms correctly; never simplify to the point of inaccuracy
- **Confident but not arrogant** — Let the evidence speak; avoid superlatives
- **Structured but not robotic** — Clear hierarchy; every sentence earns its place

## Product Identity

NAVORA is a single brand with three product surfaces:

| Product     | Audience           | Register                         |
|-------------|--------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Patient** | Patients/families  | Warm, clear, reassuring          |
| **Provider**| Clinicians         | Precise, efficient, data-dense   |
| **Nexus**   | Care coordinators  | Structured, cross-referencing    |

All three share the same token system. Differentiation comes through layout density and content, never through conflicting visual languages.
